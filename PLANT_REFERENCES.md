# Botanical references for the interactive room

The room and cutouts are generated visualizations. Plant colours are based on real taxa and cultivars, not recoloured green plants. Source photographs are references only and are not redistributed in the website.

- Caladium 'White Queen': white heart-shaped leaves, red/pink veins, green margins. https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/28234/caladium-white-queen-v/details
- Syngonium podophyllum 'Neon Robusta': pink upper leaf surfaces, green undersides, heart to arrow-shaped leaves. https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/361707/syngonium-podophyllum-neon-robusta/details
- Aglaonema 'Siam Aurora': green leaves with crimson margins and veins. https://hgic.clemson.edu/factsheet/chinese-evergreen-aglaonema-care-cultivation-growing-guide/
- Tradescantia zebrina: two silvery longitudinal bands and purple undersides. https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/79575/tradescantia-zebrina/details
- Chlorophytum comosum 'Variegatum': green strap-like leaves with white margins. https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/spider-plant-chlorophytum-comosum/

## Revised spatial composition

Each scene image layer is used once. The tabletop has one Oxalis, the left corner one tall Strelitzia, and the foreground is left clear. Five hanging plants are arranged in perspective at staggered heights; twelve different compact plants occupy the full illuminated rack.

- Strelitzia nicolai: large paddle-shaped leaves. https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/strelitzia/how-to-grow-strelitzia
- Oxalis triangularis: purple trifoliate foliage with triangular leaflets. https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/50979/oxalis-triangularis/details
- Hanging Rhipsalis baccifera: https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/14483/rhipsalis-baccifera/details
- Hanging Hoya linearis: https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/8856/hoya-linearis/details

The room now has nine stops: empty, tabletop and three small accents, side plants and shelves, Strelitzia, ceiling hangers, living wall, sparse rack, medium rack and miniature collection, fully planted rack and shelves. Three rack images are mutually exclusive and retain the same position. The room, books, vase and tray remain fixed.

Additional botanical references: Ficus pumila White Sunny (cream margins), Peperomia argyreia (silver watermelon pattern), Hypoestes (white spotting), Haworthia (succulent rosettes).
